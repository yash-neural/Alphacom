# How I used AI tools

I used **Claude AI (Claude Code)** to help with:

* **Writing code:** scaffolding the project and implementing features.
* **Test cases:** writing unit, integration, contract and MCP guardrail tests.
* **Documentation:** first drafts of the README, DECISIONS.md and SECURITY.md.
* **Debugging and running:** running the stack and tests, and fixing failures.

I made the design decisions, reviewed everything it produced, and checked the
results by running the tests and the app myself.

**OpenAI** is used only as the model inside the demo and conversation evals, not
for writing code.
